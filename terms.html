Institutional Regulations & Terms of Service

Terms & Conditions of Use

Official Departmental Regulations | Department of Statistics, Nnamdi Azikiwe University, Awka
Effective Date: Academic Session 2026/2027 | Last Revised: October 2026
Core Principle: All materials hosted on this portal (lecture notes, syllabi, statistical tables, and past examination papers) are strictly for educational and non-commercial research use by undergraduate and postgraduate students, academic researchers, and faculty. Commercial resale or unauthorized distribution is strictly prohibited.

1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing, browsing, downloading from, or utilizing the web and mobile services provided by the Department of Statistics, Faculty of Physical Sciences, Nnamdi Azikiwe University (UNIZIK) and the National Association of Students of Statistics (NASS), you agree to abide by these Terms and Conditions and all applicable University Senate regulations.

2. Permitted Academic Use

You are granted a revocable, non-exclusive, non-transferable license to access departmental educational resources under the following guidelines:

  • You may view, download, and store copies of lecture notes, past questions, and project manuals on personal devices for individual coursework, study groups, and revision.
  • You may cite curriculum guidelines and course outlines in academic papers in accordance with standard university referencing standards.
  • You may utilize Sam AI as an academic study companion to locate notes, clarify statistical concepts, and learn about department life.

3. Prohibited Activities

Users of this portal are strictly prohibited from:

  • Commercial Resale: Packaging, selling, or charging fees for past examination question papers, lecture slides, or project materials created by departmental staff.
  • Automated Scraping: Deploying denial-of-service tools, scrapers, or bots that degrade system availability for students during examination periods.
  • Impersonation & Fraud: Misrepresenting oneself as an elected NASS executive officer, lecturer, or course representative.
  • Academic Dishonesty: Using portal resources or companion tools to commit examination malpractice, plagiarism, or unauthorized communication during live tests and CBT examinations.

4. Intellectual Property

Course outlines, lecture slides, past examination question papers, and departmental emblems are the intellectual property of their respective academic authors, the Department of Statistics, and Nnamdi Azikiwe University.

The NASS UNIZIK emblem and motto ("Facts from Figures") are registered institutional marks and may not be altered or used for unauthorized commercial endorsements.

5. Administrative Updates and Official Notices

Timetable schedules, lecture venues, examination dates, and departmental announcements published on this portal are maintained by the Timetable Officer, Examination Committee, and HOD Secretariat.

While every effort is made to maintain immediate synchrony with physical noticeboards, in any event of official university circular revisions, notices issued directly by the Head of Department (HOD) or the Office of the Registrar shall remain the final institutional authority.

6. Governing Law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, the National Universities Commission (NUC) standards, and the Statutes and Ordinances of Nnamdi Azikiwe University, Awka.
