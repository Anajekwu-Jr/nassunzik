Message from the Head of Department

Advancing Statistical Science for National Development

On behalf of the academic and technical staff of the Department of Statistics, Nnamdi Azikiwe University (UNIZIK), I warmly welcome all new and returning students, researchers, and visitors to our departmental portal.

Statistics is the cornerstone of modern scientific discovery, data-driven policymaking, artificial intelligence, and economic planning. Guided by our motto, "Facts from Figures", our curriculum is rigorously crafted to equip students with mathematical mastery, probabilistic depth, and cutting-edge computational skills in R, Python, and industrial analytical tools.

This portal serves as an authoritative central repository for syllabi, past questions, lecture timetables, and SIWES manuals. We encourage every student to utilize these resources diligently as you pursue academic excellence.
