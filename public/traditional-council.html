Meet the Royal Family

About the Royal Family

The National Association of Statistics Students (NASS) Traditional Council, Nnamdi Azikiwe University, Awka, represents cultural heritage, unity, dignity, and a shared sense of identity among its members.

Rooted in the appreciation of Igbo tradition and cultural values, the royal institution is a platform for celebrating traditional titles, honouring cultural heritage, and promoting togetherness within the departmental community.

Beyond its titles, Nzenaozo stands for mutual respect, responsibility, pride in one’s heritage, and the importance of preserving traditions for future generations.

Royal History and Heritage

  • OriginThe Statistics Department had a royal lineage in the past, but its continuity was interrupted for a period. The royal tradition was re-established to continue this heritage under a new line of succession.
  • The present dynastyEstablished under HRM Igwe Egbe Ana Agba Anyanwu Nke Mbụ, personal royal name Elelie Nwa Ite Nke Mbụ na Statistics. His coronation took place on 2 July 2026.
  • Dynastic namesEach royal position has its own dynasty name, taken from its first holder. Successors inherit the name, take the next ordinal number, and may add personal names.
  • SuccessionContinuity is kept through a traditional process linked to the Ofala of the reigning Igwe. Aspiring candidates may be considered, and the dynasty names pass to the incoming holders.

Meaning of the Emblem

Each element of the emblem carries a meaning that together states the identity and values of the Council.

The Royal Crown

Traditional authority, kingship, dignity and sovereignty.

Two Eagle Feathers

Courage, strength, wisdom and excellence. The pair reflects unity and balance in leadership.

Palm Leaves

Peace, prosperity, victory, growth and abundance, and the place of the palm in Igbo life.

The Wooden Stool

The seat of authority and ancestral heritage, and the continuity of customs across generations.

The Woven Fabric

Igbo cultural identity, belonging, unity and the beauty of indigenous craftsmanship.

Black and Gold Circle

Unity and wholeness. Black for dignity and authority, gold for honour, prestige and royalty.

Red and Gold Banner

Red for courage and determination, gold for honour. It names the institution as the Traditional Council.
