Privacy Policy

Official Departmental Policy | Department of Statistics, Nnamdi Azikiwe University, Awka
Effective Date: Academic Session 2026/2027 | Last Revised: October 2026
Summary: The Department of Statistics portal is an authentic academic utility built for UNIZIK students and faculty. We do not sell user data, run third-party advertising scripts, or track your activity across the internet. Offline browser storage is used strictly for storing your academic notes, schedules, and document caches locally on your device.

1. Institutional Scope and Authority

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, use, and protection of information by the Department of Statistics, Faculty of Physical Sciences, Nnamdi Azikiwe University (UNIZIK), Awka, Anambra State, Nigeria, and the National Association of Students of Statistics (NASS). It applies to all official web, mobile, and offline portal services provided at official domains of the department.

2. Information Handled by the Portal

The portal operates primarily as a public and departmental academic repository. We minimize data handling to only what is necessary for university education:

  • Academic Curricula & Records: Public course outlines, syllabi, past examination questions, and lecturer contact directories provided for undergraduate and postgraduate training.
  • Administrative Communications: Official notices, lecture timetables, seminar schedules, and departmental announcements.
  • Student Helpdesk Inquiries: Contact information voluntarily submitted through the student helpdesk or course representatives for academic assistance.
  • Server Log Data: Standard server logs (such as request timestamps and HTTP status codes) maintained for security audit trails, load balancing, and preventing malicious activity.

3. Cookies, Caching, and Offline Storage

The portal does not use intrusive marketing cookies, behavioral tracking pixels, or commercial analytics.

We utilize standard HTML5 local storage (localStorage) and Progressive Web App (PWA) Cache API storage solely to:

  • Cache lecture notes, course outlines, and past examination papers for offline access during network instability.
  • Remember your interface preference (such as dark or light theme).
  • Cache your active level filter so you can quickly access your timetable.

You may clear your browser cache and local storage at any time through your browser settings without impacting your university academic standing.

4. Sam AI Study Companion

Sam AI is a friendly study companion for statistics students at the NASS portal designed to assist with course comprehension, syllabus navigation, and past question study.

  • Queries sent to Sam AI are processed securely through departmental server proxy endpoints solely to assist students with their coursework and department life.
  • Student queries are not used to build behavioral advertising profiles or sold to external third parties.
  • Sam AI answers strictly using approved departmental materials, lecture notes, and university records.

5. Compliance with Data Protection Regulations

The Department of Statistics processes all records in compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and Nnamdi Azikiwe University Information Technology Regulations. All student and staff records are handled with confidentiality and academic integrity.

6. Departmental Data Inquiries

For inquiries regarding data governance, student record corrections, or portal technical assistance, please contact the departmental administration:
