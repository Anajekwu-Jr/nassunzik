Departmental Examination Revision & Past Question Best Practices

Past examination question papers provided by the National Association Of Statistics Students (NASS) are designed to supplement your coursework and lecture notes. Follow these guidelines for maximum preparation efficacy:

1. Timed Simulation
Attempt past papers under strict examination conditions without checking solutions until the standard 2 to 3-hour period elapses.
2. Distribution Tables
Practice reading Normal, Student's t, Chi-Square, and F distribution tables manually to ensure speed and accuracy during the actual paper.
Download Statistical Tables (PDF)
3. Course Outline Cross-Check
Cross-reference past questions against current course outlines in the Course Catalog to account for syllabus updates.
