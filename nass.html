The NASS Mandate

Facts from Figures · Truth from Data

The National Association Of Statistics Students represents every undergraduate statistician at Nnamdi Azikiwe University. Our core mission encompasses organizing hands-on computational tutorials in R, Python, and SPSS, safeguarding student academic interests, facilitating internship linkages, and fostering camaraderie through sports and scholarly competitions.

Official Secretariat: NASS Office, Statistics Wing, Ground Floor, UNIZIK Main Campus.
