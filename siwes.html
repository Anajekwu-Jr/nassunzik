SIWES Industrial Training Manual

The definitive operational guide for Students Industrial Work Experience Scheme (SIWES) in Statistics: industry placement, logbook maintenance, technical report writing, and departmental defense.

Program Overview

Bridging Theory with Industrial Practice

The Students Industrial Work Experience Scheme (SIWES) is a compulsory skill-training program established by the Industrial Training Fund (ITF) and the National Universities Commission (NUC). For undergraduate students of Statistics at Nnamdi Azikiwe University (undertaken at the 300 Level), SIWES bridges classroom theoretical mathematical statistics with practical industry analytical workflows.

During your internship, you will participate in real-world data pipelines: data acquisition, exploratory data analysis, statistical modeling, database querying, data validation, and managerial dashboard reporting.

Key Industries for Statistics Placement

Government & Public Agencies

National Bureau of Statistics (NBS), National Population Commission (NPC), Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN), State Statistical Bureaus, and Planning Commissions.

Banking, FinTech & Insurance

Commercial banks (First Bank, Zenith, Access, GTBank), microfinance institutions, insurance actuaries, credit risk assessment units, and quantitative trading desks.

Health & Epidemiology Centers

Teaching hospitals (NAUTH), medical research institutes (NIMR), World Health Organization (WHO) field stations, and clinical trial research centers.

Technology & Market Research

Consumer research firms (Kantar, Nielsen), business intelligence software startups, telecommunications providers (MTN, Airtel), and e-commerce platforms.

The Complete 4-Step SIWES Protocol

1

Phase 1: Pre-Placement & Acceptance

Begin seeking internship placement at least 3 months before the conclusion of 300 Level first semester. Collect your official introductory letter from the Departmental SIWES Coordinator, Mr. Charles Anabike.

  • Obtain the Departmental SIWES Introductory Letter signed by the HOD and Coordinator.
  • Submit applications with tailored resumes emphasizing R, Python, SPSS, and Excel capabilities.
  • Once accepted, secure your signed & stamped Acceptance Letter and submit a copy directly to the coordinator, Mr. Charles Anabike.
  • Register your SIWES orientation biodata with the Directorate of SIWES, UNIZIK.
2

Phase 2: On-Site Training & Daily Logbook Maintenance

Your official ITF Logbook is a legal record of technical competence. Every day of your 6-month or 3-month attachment must be documented accurately with real statistical methodologies.

  • Record daily activities immediately after completion — never leave entries to memory.
  • Describe statistical procedures: data screening, normality tests, outlier treatment, hypothesis testing, survey weighting, ANOVA modeling.
  • Include sketches, formulas, R script snippets, or flowcharts in the diagram space provided.
  • Obtain the weekly signature and official rubber stamp of your industry supervisor.
Crucial Warning: Backdating entries or forging supervisor signatures is a serious academic infraction leading to immediate repetition of the SIWES unit.
3

Phase 3: Technical Report Writing

Upon completion of industrial training, each student must compile an exhaustive Technical Report following standard UNIZIK departmental formatting.

  • Chapter 1: Introduction, history, and organizational organogram of your establishment.
  • Chapter 2: Departmental units worked in and safety/security protocols.
  • Chapter 3: Technical projects, statistical tools deployed, datasets analyzed, and mathematical formulations.
  • Chapter 4: Problems encountered, skills gained, and practical recommendations for the establishment and university.
4

Phase 4: Departmental Oral Defense

Students appear before the departmental panel of academic assessors to defend their industrial experience.

  • Present a fully completed, stamped, and signed logbook alongside 3 spiral-bound copies of your report.
  • Prepare a concise 10-slide PowerPoint presentation highlighting your statistical contributions.
  • Be prepared to explain R/Python codes, formulas used, and statistical inferences drawn during your placement.
  • Dress formally in corporate business attire adhering to UNIZIK student dress codes.
