About the Department of Statistics

Pioneering mathematical depth, computational rigor, and empirical analytics in Nigeria’s premier federal institution.

Departmental Heritage & Foundations

The Department of Statistics at Nnamdi Azikiwe University (UNIZIK), Awka, is situated within the Faculty of Physical Sciences. Since its inception, the department has grown into a premier national center for statistical education, applied demographic research, biometric modeling, and econometrics.

Our programs are designed to cultivate intellectual rigor, computational agility, and ethical integrity. Today, data is the central currency of human decision-making: from biomedical clinical trials to algorithmic trading, artificial intelligence, national census tracking, and climate informatics. Our graduates lead statistical divisions in the civil service, global technology companies, and multilateral development banks.

Our Vision

To be a globally recognized center of statistical excellence, producing top-tier statisticians and data scientists capable of pioneering innovations in quantitative methodology and transforming socioeconomic development.

Our Mission

To provide robust undergraduate and postgraduate education steeped in mathematical foundations and computational technology, fostering high-impact empirical research that provides actionable solutions to industry and governance.

Academic Philosophy

Education in statistics at UNIZIK bridges foundational probability theory with practical analytical computation, training critical thinkers who generate facts from figures with integrity.

Laboratories & Facilities

Equipped with dedicated computational statistical laboratories, high-speed workstations, licensed analytical packages (R, SPSS, Python, Stata), and departmental seminar halls.
Professional Horizons & Educational Encyclopedia

Where Can Statistics Take You?

More places than you probably think.

"Statistics gives you a powerful quantitative foundation. Your career direction depends on the statistical concepts you master, the technical skills you add, your domain knowledge, your experience, and the problems you learn to solve."

Statistics is often introduced to students as the study of collecting, analysing and interpreting data. That definition is correct — but incomplete. In the modern world, Statistics sits underneath an enormous range of activities involving data, uncertainty, experimentation, prediction, and decision-making.

When a bank determines whether a customer is likely to repay a loan, when a hospital evaluates whether a treatment works, when a technology company tests a new product feature, when a government estimates unemployment, when a manufacturer monitors defects, when a researcher tests a scientific hypothesis, or when a machine-learning model learns patterns from data — statistical thinking is involved.

This means a Statistics graduate is not confined to the traditional title of "Statistician." Your degree can become the springboard for 14 major professional directions.

01 Technology & Data Statistics + Business Context + Analytical Tools

Data Analytics

Turning data into evidence for better decisions.
Link

Data Analytics is one of the most accessible modern career paths for a Statistics graduate. A data analyst takes raw or semi-structured information, investigates it, identifies patterns and trends, communicates findings, and helps an organisation make better decisions. The industry can change, but the analytical foundation travels with you.

The Intellectual Reality Check
Good analytics is not simply 'Put data into Excel and make a chart.' The statistical foundation matters. An analyst needs to understand variation, distributions, relationships, sampling, uncertainty, and the crucial difference between an apparent pattern and a meaningful, statistically significant one.

02 Technology & Data Probability & Inference + Scientific Programming + Predictive Modeling

Data Science

Statistics + Computing + Domain Knowledge.
Link

Data Science goes beyond describing what happened in the past. It investigates: What is likely to happen next? What underlying factors explain it? Can we predict it? Can we build a self-contained system that learns systematically from historical data? This is where classical Statistics intersects vigorously with computational computer science and machine learning.

The Intellectual Reality Check
A Statistics degree does NOT automatically make you a Data Scientist. Statistics gives you the mathematical intuition and inference rigor, but you must deliberately build the computational, algorithmic, and software engineering layer outside lectures.

03 Technology & Data Probability & Linear Algebra + Algorithms + Neural Frameworks + Software Engineering

Machine Learning & Artificial Intelligence

The mathematical foundation beneath modern artificial intelligence.
Link

Modern AI systems depend heavily on data. Machine learning involves algorithms learning patterns from massive datasets to make automated decisions or generate outputs. Statistics is what helps you understand what happens beneath the predictions: estimation, bias vs. variance, loss functions, uncertainty, and mathematical generalisation.

The Intellectual Reality Check
Statistics gives you the mathematical reason why models converge, but serious ML Engineering requires software engineering discipline: object-oriented programming, data structures, algorithm complexity, Docker containers, APIs, and cloud systems knowledge. You cannot just import a library; you must engineer production-grade systems.

Finance, Banking & Risk

Systems of decisions under uncertainty: where numbers meet capital.
Link

Financial systems are essentially giant engines making decisions under uncertainty. That makes Statistics extraordinarily relevant. Whether a bank is evaluating credit risk, detecting ATM card fraud, forecasting interest rate shocks, or optimizing multi-asset investment portfolios, statistical thinking drives the bottom line.

05 Finance & Risk Probability Distributions + Actuarial Mathematics + Life Contingencies + Financial Theory

Actuarial Science & Insurance

The mathematical science of long-term risk and solvency.
Link

Insurance is fundamentally about uncertainty, mortality, catastrophic events, and systemic risk. Actuaries use mathematics, statistics, and financial theory to calculate the financial impact of risk and ensure that insurance companies, pension funds, and sovereign funds remain solvent decades into the future.

The Intellectual Reality Check
A university degree in Statistics gives you an incredible head-start, but becoming a fully certified Actuary requires passing rigorous professional examinations (IFoA in the UK, SOA in the USA, or CIA). It is a demanding, prestigious, highly paid profession that requires commitment to lifelong credentialing.

06 Health & Biology Survival Analysis + Epidemiology + Experimental Design + Clinical Computing

Biostatistics & Health Data

Statistics saving lives: clinical trials, epidemiology, and public health.
Link

Statistics becomes extraordinarily powerful when applied to human health and medical science. Biostatisticians design randomized controlled trials, evaluate whether new vaccines or pharmaceuticals work, investigate disease outbreaks, measure risk factors in populations, and help prevent pandemics.

The Intellectual Reality Check
In health and medical sciences, false conclusions cost human lives. Rigour is paramount. P-values, confidence intervals, sample sizes, and blinding are not abstract academic exercises — they determine whether a drug receives clinical approval or is withdrawn from the market.

07 Economics & Policy Macro/Micro Economics + Advanced Regression + Time Series + Causal Inference

Econometrics & Economic Analysis

Empirical economics: testing economic theory with hard data.
Link

Econometrics applies statistical methods to economic questions. How does an interest rate hike affect consumer expenditure? What factors drive unemployment in emerging markets? Does an educational subsidy policy genuinely improve school outcomes? Econometricians use data to distinguish genuine economic causation from mere correlation.

The Intellectual Reality Check
Economics is not physics; you cannot easily run laboratory experiments on entire national economies. Econometricians must master observational causal inference: instrumental variables, difference-in-differences, and panel data methods to isolate true economic mechanisms.

10 Business & Industry Statistical Process Control + Design of Experiments + Reliability + Operations Research

Operations, Manufacturing & Quality Control

Statistics on the factory floor: precision, reliability, and Lean Six Sigma.
Link

Statistics isn't only about computers and office desks; it lives on the factory floor, in bottling plants, and in aviation maintenance. Manufacturing organisations use statistical process control (SPC) to monitor machinery drift, identify defect causes, eliminate process variation, and ensure millions of products meet safety specifications.

The Intellectual Reality Check
In manufacturing and engineering, you cannot inspect every single bottle of beverage or pharmaceutical tablet destructively. You must rely on rigorous acceptance sampling plans and control charts (Shewhart charts, CUSUM) to guarantee safety and compliance.

14 Health & Biology High-Dimensional Statistics + Molecular Biology + Bio-computing + Next-Gen Sequencing

Genomics & Bioinformatics

Decoding the human genome with high-dimensional statistical computing.
Link

Modern biological research produces massive datasets: billions of DNA base pairs, RNA expressions across thousands of cells, and complex gene networks. Genomics and bioinformatics use high-dimensional Statistics, multiple hypothesis testing corrections, and machine learning to identify disease-causing genetic mutations and design targeted molecular therapies.

The Intellectual Reality Check
In genomics, you often have 20,000 genes measured for only 100 patient samples — the classical 'p >> n' curse of dimensionality. Standard naive statistics collapses. You must master false discovery rates (FDR), regularization, and high-dimensional matrix methods to discover true biological signals.

UNIZIK Degree Foundation Courses

Principal Component Analysis (PCA) and clustering applied to single-cell RNA-seq.

Biological variation and quantitative genetics.
